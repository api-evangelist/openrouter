If you already have the image a video should start from, the model choice comes down to what has to happen after that frame. We compared the 26 models in our video catalog that accept a starting frame on duration, resolution, frame control, audio, reference inputs, and price per second, then show how to submit an image-to-video job with the TypeScript SDK.
