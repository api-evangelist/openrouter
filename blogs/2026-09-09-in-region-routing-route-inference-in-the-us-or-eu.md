US In-Region Routing is live, joining the EU. Send requests to us.openrouter.ai and their prompts and completions are decrypted only inside the United States and routed only to shared provider endpoints running there, across models from OpenAI, Anthropic, Google, NVIDIA, Thinking Machines, DeepSeek, Moonshot, and Qwen. Requests served by your own BYOK key go to your deployment's region, which you control.
